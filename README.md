## ⭐ Main Features

*   **🔒 Secure Login**: Your password is safe with SHA-256 encryption.
*   **📝 Manage Courses**: Easily add, edit, or delete your classes.
*   **⚡ Auto Timetable**: The system automatically finds the best schedule with **no time conflicts**.
*   **🖼️ Save as Image**: You can save your final timetable as a picture (PNG/JPG) to your phone or PC.
*   **🔍 Search & Sort**: Quickly find your courses by name or day.

---

## 🛠️ How It Works (Simple Tech)

We use professional programming methods to make the system fast and reliable:
*   **Hash Table**: For super-fast login (takes less than 1 second).
*   **Linked List**: To store and manage your list of courses.
*   **Backtracking Algorithm**: To check every possible combination and give you a schedule that works perfectly.
*   **Quick Sort**: To keep your course list organized.
